Search Engine over the UCI ICS Web Domain
UC Irvine · Search & Ranking

Full-scale search engine over 55,000+ UCI ICS web documents with an inverted index, TF-IDF ranking, PageRank authority scoring, n-gram phrase matching, and SimHash near-duplicate detection. A two-pass retrieval pipeline pools candidates quickly, then re-ranks them using positional proximity and anchor-text signals, served through a Flask web UI and validated over 40 queries.
