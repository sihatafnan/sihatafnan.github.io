Hi! I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine, advised by Dr. Habiba Farrukh. I also work closely with Unnat Jain on AI safety.

My research focuses on the security and privacy of embodied AI and other emerging systems that sense and act in the physical world, such as humanoid robots, AR/VR headsets, and autonomous vehicles. These systems constantly perceive their surroundings and the people in them, so I take a human-centered approach to securing them. I study how that perception can be manipulated by attackers, and how the data these systems collect can quietly reveal sensitive information about the people who operate and use them. My goal is to expose these risks early, through realistic attacks, measurement studies, and simulation, so they can be addressed before these systems are widely deployed.

Questions I am working on
  • Security & privacy of embodied AI: what robot learning and teleoperation data reveals about the humans who recorded it
  • AR/VR security: how a headset's own hardware can be turned against its spatial understanding
  • Autonomous driving: how physical adversarial attacks play out when a human driver is back in the loop
  • World model security: how adversaries can manipulate the learned world models that embodied agents rely on to predict, plan, and act
