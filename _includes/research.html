Who Moved the Robot? Humanoid Datasets Remember Their Operators
Sihat Afnan, Unnat Jain, Habiba Farrukh
NeurIPS 2026 Accepted

Motion retargeting, which maps a human operator's demonstrations onto a shared robot skeleton, is widely assumed to be a lossy, anonymizing projection. We show the map is effectively invertible: UNVEIL, a skeleton-aware spatiotemporal graph network, recovers operator gender, identity, and age/height from retargeted robot motion alone, generalizing to unseen operators, tasks, and retargeting implementations. Shared humanoid teleoperation datasets can therefore act as a biometric fingerprint of the people who recorded them.

Who's Watching the Road? Revisiting Physical Adversarial Attacks on Autonomous Driving in the Presence of Human Drivers
Sihat Afnan, Mutahar Ali, Chi Zhang, Praneetsai Vasu Iddamsetty, Mohammad Al Faruque, Qi Alfred Chen, Habiba Farrukh
USENIX Security 2027 Under Review

Revisits physical adversarial attacks on autonomous driving with the human driver back in the loop. Built a high-fidelity CARLA-based VR simulator integrating a full AV stack (sensor simulation, ML perception, planning, control) with real-time eye tracking and behavioral logging, and used it to measure whether and how quickly drivers notice and recover from stop-sign manipulation, lane-detection failures, and phantom obstacles.

Manipulating XR Spatial Understanding Using On-Device Speakers
Sihat Afnan, Habiba Farrukh
IEEE S&P (Oakland) 2027 Under Review

The first on-device acoustic attack on XR spatial understanding: an optimization framework drives a headset's own built-in speakers to perturb camera odometry, RGB imaging, and depth sensing, producing controlled geometric distortions in the reconstructed 3D map with no added hardware. Demonstrated object addition/removal, surface misclassification, and degraded user task performance across Meta Quest 3S, Apple iPad, and Project Aria glasses, corrupting over 91% of spatial maps in real-world Quest 3S trials.

LogShield: A Transformer-based Advanced Persistent Threat Detection System Leveraging Self-Attention
Sihat Afnan*, Mushtari Sadia*, Shahrear Iqbal, Anindya Iqbal (*equal contribution)
arXiv 2023

A self-attention framework for detecting APT patterns in event sequences derived from system provenance graphs, reaching F1 scores of 98% and 95% on DARPA OpTC and DARPA TC E3, compared with 96% and 94% for LSTM baselines.
