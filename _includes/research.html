Who Moved the Robot? Humanoid Datasets Remember Their Operators
Sihat Afnan, Unnat Jain, Habiba Farrukh
NeurIPS 2026 Accepted

Motion retargeting, which maps a human operator's demonstrations onto a shared robot skeleton, is widely assumed to be a lossy, anonymizing projection. We show the map is effectively invertible: UNVEIL, a skeleton-aware spatiotemporal graph network, recovers operator gender, identity, and age/height from retargeted robot motion alone, generalizing to unseen operators, tasks, and retargeting implementations. Shared humanoid teleoperation datasets can therefore act as a biometric fingerprint of the people who recorded them.

LogShield: A Transformer-based Advanced Persistent Threat Detection System Leveraging Self-Attention
Sihat Afnan*, Mushtari Sadia*, Shahrear Iqbal, Anindya Iqbal (*equal contribution)
arXiv 2023

A self-attention framework for detecting APT patterns in event sequences derived from system provenance graphs, reaching F1 scores of 98% and 95% on DARPA OpTC and DARPA TC E3, compared with 96% and 94% for LSTM baselines.
